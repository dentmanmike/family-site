A few honest answers

Can I call during the school day?

The launch concept is scheduled evenings and weekends only. Requests may be submitted once a real intake channel is configured, but daytime response is not guaranteed. Businesses that need immediate daytime assistance should use a staffed provider.

Is this a full managed service provider?

Not at launch. These are limited support and maintenance offers. A full MSP model needs reliable daytime coverage, monitoring, security and backup tooling, escalation partners and documented service commitments before being advertised.

Is remote access always on?

No unattended remote access is assumed. The proposed starting model uses client-authorized sessions. Any persistent agent or unattended access requires explicit written consent, a secured tool and defined removal/offboarding procedures.

What happens in a security emergency?

This service does not provide emergency incident response. Suspected ransomware, account compromise or urgent business outages need a qualified incident-response or staffed IT provider. An escalation partner must be arranged before any managed-response promise is sold.

What if I need new hardware or a bigger project?

Purchases, migrations, major repairs and infrastructure projects are quoted separately. You approve the estimate first. Work outside Mike’s scope is declined or referred to an appropriate specialist.

Can I cancel monthly care?

The recommended launch model is month-to-month with 30 days’ written notice. This is a proposed term only; the signed agreement will define cancellation, access removal, documentation handoff and any outstanding charges.
